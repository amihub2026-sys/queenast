Let’s plan your visit ↗
How do I book an appointment?

Choose your service and preferred date in the booking form. It opens WhatsApp with your details; the salon will confirm availability directly.

Can you help me choose a service?

Yes. Share your preferences and concerns during your consultation so we can discuss suitable options with you.

What should I bring for bridal makeup?

Bring your outfit details, jewellery references and inspiration pictures. Contact the salon beforehand to discuss timing and preparation.

Is my appointment confirmed after submitting?

Your form sends a request. Your booking is confirmed only once the salon replies and agrees on a date and time.

How can I change my appointment?

Contact the salon on WhatsApp with your name and appointment details as early as possible to discuss a new time.
